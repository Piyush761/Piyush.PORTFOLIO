Business Problem: Analyzed a retail dataset of 10,194 order lines across 5,111 distinct orders to evaluate profitability, customer behavior, and regional performance, culminating in an interactive dashboard for stakeholders.

Key Insights:

  • West Region leads in both total sales and profitability.
  • Central Region struggles with the lowest regional profit margin.
  • Technology is the most profitable category, while Furniture suffers from relatively low margins.
  • Tables represent the largest loss-making sub-category, requiring immediate pricing or supply chain review.
  • High customer revenue does not strictly correlate with positive profitability; sales volume and profit margins must be evaluated jointly.
